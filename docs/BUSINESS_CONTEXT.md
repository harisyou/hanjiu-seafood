# Haris OS Business Context

Haris OS is not a generic ecommerce website. It is an operating system for the
real sales and fulfillment workflow of a seafood business.

## Current business workflow

```text
Fish market
    ↓
Photography
    ↓
Product creation
    ↓
Facebook
    ↓
LINE
    ↓
Threads
    ↓
Customer orders
    ↓
Packing
    ↓
Delivery
```

Seafood supply, weights, prices, and availability can change daily. Staff must
photograph new products, publish them quickly, answer customers across social
channels, record accurate orders, prepare each order, and complete delivery.
Many sales begin as conversations rather than conventional web checkouts.

Haris OS should reduce repeated data entry and uncertainty across this chain.
The product must help staff move from market intake to a fulfilled customer
order with fewer steps and clearer information.

## Decision priority

Business workflow optimization has higher priority than adding new technology.
A new framework, AI feature, service, or abstraction is valuable only when it
measurably improves speed, accuracy, customer service, or operational control.
Technical novelty alone is not a product requirement.

When priorities conflict, prefer:

1. Accurate orders and preserved business history.
2. Faster daily operations.
3. Clear customer communication.
4. Mobile usability in real working conditions.
5. Simple systems the team can operate and maintain.
