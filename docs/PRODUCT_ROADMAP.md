# Haris OS Product Roadmap

The roadmap is ordered by operational value. Dates and sequence can change as
the seafood business reveals higher-priority workflow needs.

## Milestone M1 — Commerce foundation

- Product Management
- Product Variants
- Shopping Cart
- Orders
- Dashboard

M1 establishes a reliable path from product creation to order fulfillment, with
mobile-friendly daily administration.

## Milestone M2 — Operations and customer growth

- Restaurant Mode
- Wholesale Mode
- CRM
- Batch Price Editing
- Inventory Management

M2 supports distinct buyer workflows, repeat customers, high-volume catalog
updates, and traceable stock operations.

## Milestone M3 — Intelligence and business control

- AI Content
- AI Pricing
- Analytics
- Finance
- AI Business Assistant

M3 uses the structured operational history from M1 and M2 to improve decisions.
AI features must assist human operators, expose their assumptions, and never
silently change prices, inventory, orders, or financial records.
