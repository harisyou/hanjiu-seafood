# Changelog

All notable changes to Haris OS are documented here.

## v0.3.0

### Added

- Customer-facing seafood product catalog.
- Supabase-backed product and order data.
- Administrative authentication.
- Product creation, editing, status control, sorting, and featured products.
- Product image upload to Supabase Storage.
- Client-side image validation, resizing, and WebP compression.
- Customer order form and administrative order-status management.
- Product variant data model and management workflow foundation.
- Variant selection, variant pricing, availability labels, and variant-aware cart
  and order-item design.
- Engineering documentation foundation for architecture, product direction,
  business context, UX, and data preservation rules.

### Improved

- Mobile layouts for customer and administrative workflows.
- Production build compatibility and TypeScript correctness.
- Protection of product image upload, login, product management, and order
  management flows while extending the catalog.
